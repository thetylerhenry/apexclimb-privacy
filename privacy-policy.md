# Apex Climb Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 24, 2026*

Tyler Jairus Henry LLC ("we," "us") makes Apex Climb, an iOS game. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and your choices. Questions? Email [CONTACT EMAIL].

## 1. Who can play

Apex Climb is rated 17+ on the App Store. The real-money tournament is open only to players who are 18 or older and located in eligible U.S. states. Each tournament has a $5 entry fee, and no tournament starts until 100 players have joined. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (see Section 7).

## 2. What we collect

- **Account info:** your player name and a player ID. If you join the tournament, we also collect your legal name, email, date of birth, and the details needed to pay prizes, such as a payout account and any tax information the law requires.
- **Gameplay data:** scores, altitude, tier, coins collected, power-ups used, daily streak check-ins, unlocked looks, lives, and the time you joined the joining board.
- **Purchase history:** what you bought and when, including life packs ($4.99, $7.99, $14.99), the optional Camp Kit ($1.99), joining-board spot buyouts ($50), and tournament entries ($5 minimum). Apple processes all App Store payments, so we never see your full card number.
- **Location:** only when you enter or play the real-money tournament. We check it to confirm you're in an eligible U.S. state (geo-fencing). We use approximate or precise location just for that check and don't track you in the background.
- **Device info:** device model, iOS version, app version, language, and crash or performance logs.
- **Fair-play data:** input timing and run statistics we use to detect cheating and bots.

## 3. How we use it

- To run the game: saving your progress, streaks, coins, lives, and unlocks.
- To show the worldwide leaderboard and the joining board.
- To deliver purchases and restore them.
- To run the tournament: checking eligibility and location, verifying winners, and paying prizes ($50, $40, $30, $20, and $10 to the top five).
- To prevent cheating, fraud, and abuse.
- To fix bugs and improve the game.
- To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we don't use it for third-party advertising.

## 4. What other players can see

Your player name, score, and rank appear on the worldwide leaderboard. Your player name and join order appear on the joining board. Pick a player name that doesn't reveal your real identity.

## 5. Who we share it with

- **Apple:** App Store purchases, refunds, and Game Center (if you use it).
- **[PAYOUT PROVIDER]:** to pay tournament prizes and verify identity.
- **[HOSTING / BACKEND PROVIDER]:** to store leaderboard, account, and anti-cheat data.
- **Legal and safety:** when the law requires it, or to protect players and the game.
- **Business transfer:** if we merge with or sell to another company, your data goes to that company under this policy.

Bitrise (our app-building service) and GitHub (our code storage) are tools we use to build the app. They don't receive your player data.

## 6. How long we keep it

We keep your gameplay and account data while your account is active. We keep tournament, payout, and purchase records as long as tax and legal rules require, typically up to 7 years. We keep location checks only as long as we need them to confirm eligibility for that entry. Anti-cheat records are kept as long as needed to investigate abuse.

## 7. Children's privacy (COPPA)

Apex Climb is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, and the tournament is 18+ only. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email [CONTACT EMAIL] and we'll delete it.

## 8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

- Access a copy of your data.
- Correct inaccurate data.
- Delete your account and data. You can do this in Settings or by email, but we'll keep records we're legally required to hold.
- Turn off location access in iOS Settings. Without it, you can't enter the tournament, but you can still play everything else.

California and other U.S. state residents may have additional rights under their state laws, including the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the sale or sharing of their information. We don't sell or share personal information for advertising. To make a request, email [CONTACT EMAIL]. We'll respond within 45 days and won't treat you differently for using your rights.

## 9. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls, and Apple's secure payment system. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your device protected.

## 10. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. For big changes, we'll also let you know in the app.
