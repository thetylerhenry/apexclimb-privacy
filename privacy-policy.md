# Apex Climb Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 24, 2026*

Tyler Jairus Henry LLC ("we," "us") makes Apex Climb, an iOS game. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, who can see it, and your choices. Questions? Email tylerjairushenryllc@gmail.com.

## 1. Who can play

Apex Climb is rated 17+ on the App Store. Any feature we limit by age is open only to players in the U.S. who are 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (see Section 9).

## 2. What the game uses

- **Game Center profile:** your Game Center name and player ID, used for the worldwide leaderboard.
- **Gameplay data:** scores, altitude, tier, player level and XP, coins, power-ups, daily streaks, lives, play time, and the cosmetics you own and equip.
- **Your flag:** the country flag you choose to represent yourself. You can pick any country's flag, including Palestine, or none.
- **Player of the Day posts:** if you buy a Player of the Day post, we store your Game Center player ID, display name, flag, equipped emblem, your message, the Apple transaction ID for that purchase, and when the post starts and expires.
- **Reports and blocks:** your player ID, the posts you report, the reason you pick, and the players you block.
- **Parental control settings:** whether the spending limit or no-spend setting is on, a protected (hashed) parent PIN, and the day's purchase total.
- **Purchase history:** what you bought and when (see Section 4). Apple processes all payments, so we never see your card number.
- **Messages you send us:** if you email us, we get your email address and whatever you write.

The game doesn't ask for or use your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

## 3. How we use it

- To run the game: saving your progress, streaks, coins, lives, cosmetics, and settings.
- To show the worldwide leaderboard and the Player of the Day, and to check Player of the Day purchases with Apple.
- To deliver and track purchases and free rewards.
- To enforce parental controls.
- To filter offensive words, handle reports, and prevent cheating and abuse.
- To answer your emails and fix bugs.

We do not sell your personal information, and we don't use it for third-party advertising.

## 4. Purchases, cosmetics, and free rewards

**In-app purchases:**
- Life packs: $4.99, $7.99, and $14.99.
- Camp Kit: $1.99.
- Cosmetic shop: five rarity tiers.
  - Mythic: $10
  - Legendary: $8
  - Rare: $5
  - Uncommon: $1.99
  - Common: $1.00
- Player of the Day post: $5.00.

Cosmetics (character skins, emblems, and trails) change how you look only. They don't give a gameplay advantage.

**Random items:** Buying a cosmetic tier gives you a random item from that tier that you don't already own, so you never get duplicates. The odds of getting each item are shown before you buy. Items you haven't unlocked appear grayed out with a "?" in the collection until you get them.

**Level-up rewards:** You earn XP as you climb (1 XP for every 10 meters), and leveling up gives free cosmetics:
- Levels 2 through 9: a free Common or Uncommon, followed by an optional offer to buy one specific Rare item shown on screen.
- Level 10: a free Rare, followed by an optional offer to buy one specific Mythic item shown on screen. That Mythic is not free.
- Levels 15 and 20: a free Rare.
- Every other level above 10: a free Uncommon, plus optional offers for higher tiers with odds shown.
- Before level 10, you won't see Legendary or Mythic offers.

When an offer shows a specific item, buying it gives you exactly that item. Levels 1 through 10 come quickly, and each level after 10 takes longer to reach.

**Free rewards:** We also give free cosmetics as you play:
- A free Common about every 2 minutes of active play.
- A free Common each time you lose a run.
- A free Rare about once per hour of total play, and occasionally at other times.
- Players who haven't made a purchase may get random free Commons and Uncommons. They don't see Legendary offers.
- Players who have made a purchase may see offers for the Legendary tier.

Mythic and Legendary items are never free. Rare items are mostly purchased. We use your purchase history, stored on your device, to decide which offers you see.

## 5. Parental controls

Parents can turn on these controls in Settings, under Parental Control:
- **Limit Spending** caps in-app purchases at $50 per day.
- **Can't Spend on This Account** blocks all in-app purchases.

Both are locked behind a 4-digit parent PIN. When a limit is reached or purchases are off, the game blocks the purchase and says so. These settings are stored only on the device. Deleting the app resets them, and we can't recover a forgotten PIN. Parents can also use Apple's Screen Time and Ask to Buy.

## 6. What other players can see

Other players can see:
- On the worldwide leaderboard: your Game Center name, score, rank, flag, and equipped emblem.
- If you post a Player of the Day message: your name, flag, emblem, and message, for up to 24 hours.

Pick a name and message that don't reveal your real identity.

## 7. Player of the Day messages, reports, and blocking

Any player can buy a Player of the Day post for $5.00. Your message, up to 80 characters, is shown to all players for 24 hours, or until another player buys a post, which replaces it. Every message goes through a word filter in the app before you pay and again on our server, which rejects profanity and slurs, including disguised spellings. If our server rejects a message, you can submit new text without paying again. We check each purchase with Apple before posting, and Apple handles refunds.

On any Player of the Day post, you can:
- **Report** it as offensive, harassment or bullying, spam, or other. A reported post is hidden for you, and a post reported by three different players is hidden for everyone.
- **Block** the player, which hides their posts for you. You can unblock them in Settings.

To report abuse directly, email **tylerjairushenryllc@gmail.com**. We review reports promptly and remove posts that break our rules. A removed or replaced post is not refunded by us; refund requests go through Apple.

## 8. Who we share it with

- **Apple:** App Store purchases, refunds, Game Center, and Screen Time. To confirm a Player of the Day purchase, our server checks the purchase with Apple.
- **Supabase:** our backend service. It stores Player of the Day posts, reports, and blocks, and delivers the current Player of the Day message to every player, on our behalf. It may only use this data to provide that service.
- **Legal and safety:** when the law requires it, or to protect players and the game.
- **Business transfer:** if we merge with or sell to another company, your data goes to that company under this policy.

We don't use third-party analytics or advertising services. Bitrise (our app-building service) and GitHub (our code storage) are tools we use to build the app. They don't receive your player data.

## 9. Children's privacy (COPPA)

Apex Climb is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email tylerjairushenryllc@gmail.com and we'll delete it.

## 10. How long we keep it

Most game data stays on your device until you delete the app. Player of the Day posts are shown for up to 24 hours. We keep posts, reports, and blocks on Supabase for up to 1 year to handle abuse reports, purchase questions, and safety, then delete them. Game Center leaderboard scores are kept by Apple under Apple's policies. We keep emails and abuse reports only as long as we need them to respond and keep the game safe, or as the law requires.

## 11. Your rights

You can:
- **Delete your local data** by deleting the app.
- **Remove your flag** by choosing "none" in Settings.
- **Manage Game Center** in iOS Settings.
- **Ask us** to access, correct, or delete any information we hold about you by emailing tylerjairushenryllc@gmail.com.

California and other U.S. state residents may have additional rights under their state laws, including the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the sale or sharing of their information. We don't sell or share personal information for advertising. We'll respond within 45 days and won't treat you differently for using your rights.

## 12. Security

We protect the parent PIN with a salted hash, use Apple's secure payment system, keep most data on your device, and store Player of the Day data on Supabase with access limited to our server. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your device protected.

## 13. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. For big changes, we'll also let you know in the app.
